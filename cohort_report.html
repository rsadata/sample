Time-to-Click Analysis

What is time_to_click_days?  For every student action (View, Bookmark, Apply, Interview, Offer), we record the difference between when the job was posted and when the student's status event occurred. A lower number means students are finding and acting on fresh job postings faster — a leading indicator of platform engagement quality.
